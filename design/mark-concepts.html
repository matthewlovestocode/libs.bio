A mark for libs.bio: four concepts

Each is drawn in the same language as the Bionet mark: one thin line weight, round ends, cyan with a single accent. The accent here is the theme's green, which already means "good", for libraries whose claim is that they are verified. Each tile shows the mark large, at favicon size, and beside the name.

For reference: the Bionet mark

A lower-case b whose bowl is a plasmid in three arcs. Cyan is yours, violet is another lab's.

1. The dot is a plasmid

A lower-case l and the dot from the name, drawn as a small plasmid. It is Bionet's b taken apart: the same stem, the same ring, set side by side.

2. The ladder

An l with the bands of a DNA ladder beside it, spaced unevenly as they are on a real gel. One band is the verified one.

3. The strand

An l drawn as a short stretch of double-stranded DNA: two strands and three rungs. Linear, where Bionet's is circular.

4. A call on a plasmid

A plasmid between brackets, as it would sit in a line of code. The most "library" of the four, and the least like a letter.
