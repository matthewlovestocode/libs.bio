Bionet panels: three treatments

One Freezer card and the Lab tab's floating controls, drawn over the lab scene. Colours are the Bionet theme's own. Only the panel fill, edge and blur change between rows.

1. Solid, as today

Opaque fill and a plain border. Nothing behind the panel is visible.

2. Subtle frost

About 72% opaque with a 12px blur. The scene reads as soft colour behind the panel.

3. Pronounced glass

About 10% tint in dark and 36% in light, a 22px blur, a bright edge and a top highlight.
